Where the money comes back

Most Mars cost is not the last pound on the lander. It is unique design, unused flown mass, and loss sized to a whole module. A and P attacks those three lines.

Without A and P

  • Each crate is its own qualified article
  • Load plans do not share an index
  • Optional science rides because it was already designed
  • One failed entry writes off a two-ton object

With A and P

  • Design the interface once. Rows share n and P
  • Filter class before you spend NRE
  • Leave Optional on Earth until Core works
  • A loss event is sized to one catalog pound, not a habitat
